Design Choices & UX Challenges Report

Word Count: ~440 words • Currency: Philippine Pesos (₱) • Mobile-First + PC Architecture

1. Chosen Key User Flow: Artisanal Beverage Ordering

The prototype models the quintessential user journey for an artisanal coffee roastery ("Velvet & Bean"). The flow progresses smoothly through four cohesive milestones: Menu Discovery → Beverage Customization → Cart Review & Checkout → Live Order Tracking. This sequence captures all primary touchpoints, balancing rich visual appeal with frictionless transactional utility.

2. Design Choices: Typography, Color Palette & Visual Hierarchy

To evoke the warmth and craft of an upscale cafe, the color scheme employs deep espresso charcoal (#1A110B), roasted bronze accents (#D97736), and creamy oat-milk background layers (#FAF7F2). Accents in fresh sage green (#3F8A58) provide intuitive cues for positive system statuses. All pricing is standardized in Philippine Pesos (₱) to align with local market ergonomics.

Typography pairs Outfit for bold display titles and numerical pricing with Plus Jakarta Sans for clean, legible micro-controls. Visual hierarchy is established through distinct typographic scaling, high-contrast badges (e.g., "Best Seller"), and an 8-point spatial layout ensuring minimum 44px touch targets.

3. Responsive Architecture: Mobile-First & Desktop PC View

The application follows an uncompromising mobile-first responsive architecture:

  • Mobile Viewport: Features single-column touch navigation, native bottom sheet customizer drawer, floating order tray, and thumb-accessible bottom tabs.
  • Desktop PC View: Expands naturally into an expansive multi-column desktop storefront with a persistent 2-column layout (wide menu grid alongside a sticky live order summary sidebar), desktop top header, and centered dialog modals.

4. Interactive Transitions & Micro-Animations

The prototype incorporates four distinct animations that elevate perceived performance:

  • Bottom-Sheet & Modal Spring Easing: Opening the customizer utilizes a fluid cubic-bezier curve (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) with contextual backdrop blur.
  • Spatial Screen Transitions: Smooth page shifts maintain cognitive orientation between Menu, Checkout, and Live Tracker.
  • Brewing Radar & Live Timeline: Pulsing concentric radar waves and an animated timeline milestone bar visually reassure customers as their drink is prepared.
  • Dynamic Price Recalculation: Cup size, milk, and add-on modifiers calculate totals live in Philippine Pesos with tactile button feedback.

5. Design Challenges & Solutions Overcome

A major challenge was mitigating cognitive fatigue during beverage customization. Specialty coffee presents numerous interdependent variables (size, milk alternatives, sugar %, ice, toppings). This was solved using segmented chips with transparent upfront pricing (e.g., +₱30) and live total recalculation. Another challenge was preventing post-checkout anxiety; implementing a real-time milestone tracker with animated brewing indicators provides psychological reassurance and trust.
